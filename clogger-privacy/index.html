Clogger Privacy Policy

eviltim studios · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Clogger is a free game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game collects nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Clogger has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any data to eviltim studios or to anyone else, and it has no server of its own.

What stays on your device

Your progress (skins, coins, trophies, settings and high scores) is saved on your phone only. We never see it.

Android backup

If Android's backup feature is turned on for your phone, Android may copy the game's save data to your own Google Drive and restore it on a new phone or after a reinstall. This is part of Android and your Google account, controlled by you in your phone's Settings, and governed by Google's privacy policy. eviltim studios does not receive or have access to these backups.

Permissions

The game uses your phone's vibration for haptic feedback, if you leave Vibration on in Settings. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Because Clogger collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game to remove its saved data from your phone. To remove an Android backup, use the backup settings of your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.
